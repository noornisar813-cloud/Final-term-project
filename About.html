About Urban'Pick

URBAN’PICK is a modern lifestyle and fashion brand dedicated to bringing you trend-forward clothing, handcrafted jewelry, and everyday essentials that define urban elegance. Born from a love of creativity and individuality, URBAN’PICK celebrates self-expression through minimal yet bold designs — from streetwear classics to artisanal accessories. Every piece is curated with quality, comfort, and confidence in mind, so you can wear your style wherever life takes you.

Our Story

Urban Picks started with a small dream and a big heart. We just wanted to make kids smile. From simple ideas to real products — we worked hard to blend colours and joy into our designs that could light up a child’s face. We Dreamed It. We Built It. But what makes us different? Our core idea was to make everyday school essentials fun, stylish, and full of life. We realized that kids love products that reflect their personality – colorful school bags, fancy bottles, and stationery that stand out. That’s why we created Urban Picks – a brand that brings smiles, confidence, and joy to kids with products designed just for them. We promise to deliver products that inspire happiness and creativity. Urban Picks is not just about fun; it’s about function, trust, and care.
